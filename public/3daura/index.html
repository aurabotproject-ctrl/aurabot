💡 How team worlds work.
  • You can be in one team world at a time, with up to 5 builders.
  • When you join, your own world is put away safely — nothing is deleted.
  • Leave whenever you like and your own world comes straight back, exactly as you left it.

  • Your pets always stay with you, wherever you build.
